# Student User Guide

## 1. Registering Attendance
1. Sign in to your student portal.
2. When your teacher starts class attendance, an active session indicator appears on your dashboard.
3. Open the **Camera Scanner** to scan the live rotating QR code on the classroom monitor, or enter the **6-digit challenge code**.
4. If location verification is required, tap **Verify GPS** to grant permission.
5. Tap **Submit Attendance Check-In**.

## 2. Attendance Status & History
- **Verified**: Attendance recorded and confirmed.
- **Pending Review**: Attendance recorded and awaiting teacher visual confirmation.
- **Rejected**: Challenge code expired or verification failed. Scan the current live code to retry.
- View your daily, weekly, and monthly attendance percentage from your dashboard at any time.
